### Galahax
A Galaga-style shooter game about destroying hacker ships. Made completely in Python using Pygame. 

You are an electrical plug trying to protect against electrical components shorting the socket. There are a variety of components, but some other objects want to short the socket too. Use your power to overload and destroy the components. 

**How To Play**

Use the arrow keys or WASD to move. Space or Enter to shoot. Works with number pad.

### Self Sabotage
Self Sabotage is a game where you start as a cannon and shoot into "The Box". Then you become the player and have to dodge your shots within "The Box". Remember where you put your shots!!! In later levels, there are also mines and bouncing shots. Made in Python using Pygame, images made in Microsoft Paint.

**How To Play**

Use the arrow keys or WASD to move. Left-click to shoot and place mines. Works with number pad.

### Disaster Relief
Disaster Relief is a tower defense game where you are trying to enjoy your grass lawn, but the elements keep taking it over. Using various towers, defend your lawn from the greedy elements. Made in Python using Pygame.

**How To Play**

Buy a Tower From the shop and place it on a tile. As you destroy tiles you will get more coins, use them to buy more and better towers from the shop.

### Fall Farmer
Fall Farmer is a game where fruit magically falls from the gods in the sky, and you must collect it in your basket to make money. The fruit isn't free; after all, you must show appreciation to the gods.... Made in Python using Pygame.

**How To Play**

Use the arrow keys or A and D to move and catch apples. Works with number pad. Choose your upgrade from the shop using left-click.
